# Rights, Release and Monetization

## Boundary

This repository produces original music packets and verified local generation receipts. It does not automatically create a releasable master, establish copyright ownership, grant a commercial license, submit to a distributor or publish to a platform.

## Product lines

- Creator Producer Pack: production profile, skills, templates and training examples.
- Score and Sound Kits: original cues, stems, loops, MIDI/MusicXML and production notes under explicit licenses.
- Guided Focus Worlds: original ambient beds combined with journals, meditations and visual experiences; creative wellness only.
- Direct-to-fan releases: tracks, albums, soundtracks, process notes and optional physical editions.
- Cinematic experience licenses: music paired with Arcanea or Starlight web, learning and product experiences.
- Premium direction: bounded bespoke composition or creative direction at a scope and price that protects human time.

## Rights gate

For each work record contributors and roles, composition ownership, master ownership, lyric authorship, voice/model consent, samples, source/tool provenance, splits, territory, term, exclusivity, allowed/prohibited uses, attribution, release authority and takedown route. Keep private lossless masters and stems separate from public compressed renditions.

No named-artist imitation, cloned voice, borrowed lyric, hidden sample or promise of rights beyond the actual provider terms and contributor agreements.

## Channel order

1. Owned catalog and rights ledger.
2. Bandcamp for direct-to-fan releases and durable downloads.
3. BeatStars for explicitly licensed beats, sound kits or collections.
4. DSPs such as Spotify through a reviewed distributor only after metadata, identifiers and rights are complete.
5. Sync, games, apps, education and cinematic web through explicit use-specific licenses.

Streaming is discovery and continuity, not the sole revenue plan. Prioritize direct sales, reusable licenses, bundles and recurring libraries where they preserve customer and creator agency.
