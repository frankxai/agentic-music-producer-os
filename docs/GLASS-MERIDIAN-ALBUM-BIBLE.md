# Glass Meridian — Private Album Bible

**Status:** private production plan; no publication/distribution/monetization authorization.

## Central creative decision

Make a 10-track instrumental night-drive album in which a close piano and solo violin carry the human line while chamber-orchestral color and disciplined festival-scale low end widen the horizon without crowding the emotional detail.

## Listener and emotional arc

A listener is moving through a city after an overloaded day: **T01–02** locate and clear the frame; **T03–05** build momentum and collective scale; **T06** creates the lone pause; **T07–09** release and widen the horizon; **T10** lands quietly rather than manufacturing triumph.

## Production grammar

- **Core:** felt piano attacks, solo violin as the melodic witness, restrained viola/cello support, occasional horn/clarinet air, controlled electronic kick/sub-bass.
- **Beat rule:** each dance-scale track has one clear low-end owner at a time: kick or sub-bass, never a permanently stacked low-end wash.
- **Chamber rule:** strings answer or frame the piano; they do not pad every bar. Protect a dry midrange lane for piano transient and violin articulation.
- **Festival rule:** impact comes from contrast—short silence, filtered re-entry, octave lift, or drum subtraction—not from an endless wall of cymbals, risers, choirs, or drops.
- **Vocal rule:** all tracks are instrumental; Custom Mode `Instrumental` stays on. The voice-observation contract is therefore not applicable. No vocal/persona selection is inferred.
- **Frequency-motif policy:** `528`, `936`, `1100`, and fast amplitude-modulation/gamma-rate ideas are optional *aesthetic motif labels only*. Prompts may say “a subtle sine-color or high harmonic shimmer” but never promise an exact frequency. Spectrum analysis after an authorized download is the only way to describe an actual observed component. No effect claim of any kind is allowed.
- **Prompt rule:** genre first; then emotional energy, tempo/pocket, focal instruments, dynamic arc, spatial behavior, ending. No named artists, songs, franchises, or clone language.

## Album-wide reject conditions

Reject any take with a generic long intro, constant string pad, muddy piano/violin midrange, a generic festival drop, unprompted choir, overdriven low end, abrupt unresolved ending, audible vocal fragments, or a claimed/assumed exact frequency component. `KEEP` requires actual playback, one clearly identifiable opening, a memorable instrumental nucleus, an earned contrast, and a usable ending.

- **BPM/key/map:** 124 BPM, D minor to suspended F major; 8-bar piano/violin opening (2) → A section (4) → filtered lift (6) → wide chorus/drop (8) → stripped violin bridge (3) → final return (9) → 4-bar piano release (2).

- **STYLE:** `Chamber-electronic progressive house; clear nocturnal resolve; 124 BPM in a straight, restrained four-on-the-floor pocket; felt piano, close solo violin, cello ostinato, chamber strings, tight kick and controlled sub-bass with open verse midrange; instrumental, no vocals; intimate opening to a wide but disciplined main-room lift, stripped bridge, final octave return; dry piano transient with wide string halo and clean center image; end on a held piano octave and sub-bass release.`

## Review gate

Each track must independently receive a reviewed packet score of at least **85/100**, no axis below **7.5/10**, and no hard veto before one serialized Create authorization. The album maximum is **10 Create actions** total; one action is reserved for one fully reviewed track only. A generation card is not a listening result, a download requires its own take-linked authorization, and `KEEP` is unavailable until actual playback is logged.
