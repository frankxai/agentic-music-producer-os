# Customer edition — offer and adoption contract

**Buyer:** independent musician or producer with scattered ideas/takes/tools who wants one repeatable route from a brief to a reviewed recording and a staged release campaign.

**Promise:** configure an artist/album workspace, preserve exact decisions and recordings, budget the campaign, and prepare a traceable release/marketing packet. Streaming demand, legal clearance, revenue and an autonomous label are outcomes to develop, not guaranteed deliverables.

**Primary price hypothesis:** EUR 99 one-time for one creator/business workspace. Brand: Starlight's Agentic Music Producer OS; Arcanea Records is the private operated showcase, FrankX the creator-education/distribution voice. Keep the existing product_id and sku_id; do not create another product identity.

## Package

Six connected Notion databases: Artists, Albums & Campaigns, Recordings, Knowledge & Experiments, Cost Ledger and Actions.
Twelve lifecycle SOPs; five campaign strategies; human/hybrid/generated production paths; simple/score-first/explicit-lyrics distinctions; research/knowledge-card protocol; cover and video briefs; release/rights/metadata evidence; marketing and ad planning; offline budget checker; fixed-allowlist draft exporter; fictional worked campaign; AI operating instructions; changelog and migration guide.

The existing portable Hermes profile is an optional technical execution path. Its machine prerequisites, installed readiness and advanced packet contract must be inspected separately. The Notion/playbook product requires neither Hermes nor private Songcraft repository access. Included instructions are not connected API accounts or hosted autonomous workers.

## Activation

First 15 minutes: replace the fictional artist, enter one listener moment, choose a production path, create one exact compact brief, choose a campaign strategy and set a cash allowance.
First 60–90 minutes (planning target): link one owned recording or explicitly mark awaiting_render, capture one real review if audio is available, stage one campaign and list its unresolved gates.
First complete outcome: one chosen actual recording with recoverable provenance and a staged packet, achieved through the buyer's tools and real work.

A prepared packet without audio counts only as a planning activation. Product analytics must distinguish first brief, first real listening receipt and first staged campaign. These times are onboarding targets, not tested performance promises.

## Account and tool prerequisites

Notion workspace; authorized music and art tools; private file/evidence storage; optional Python 3.11+ for the planner; paid generation/export entitlement when applicable; distributor and channel accounts only when the buyer reaches those stages. Paid subscriptions, credits, storage, ad spend and licensing services are separately funded.

## License, updates and support — proposal

One creator/business workspace, editable for internal use; buyer owns their original inputs/outputs subject to tool/contributor rights. No redistribution of the paid template, source material or another artist's corpus. This is proposed commercial scope, not an activated license; repository/dependency/asset rights and actual terms need review before listing.

Proposed 12 months of compatible pack updates; major hosted services or a new product are separate. Written setup guide, troubleshooting and batch support; no bespoke implementation, unlimited calls, community management or account operation included. Track support minutes and fix recurring onboarding friction in the product.

## Clean export rule

Build only fixed generic files. Never copy an operated workspace, query an artist catalog or sanitize it with a blacklist alone. The offline exporter accepts no customer/artist input path. It produces a deterministic ZIP with file hashes and draft/license status, refuses overwrite and excludes unlisted files. Content review still matters: an allowlist cannot judge every sentence added to a listed guide.

Notion customer edition is a separate private draft with child-owned databases and fictional data. Duplicate the complete root, verify relations remap to the customer's copies, then inspect sharing. Linked views into the operator's private databases are forbidden. The duplication/permission/mobile tests must pass before public template delivery.

## Versioning and migration

Patch: documentation/freshness corrections. Minor: compatible field/workflow additions. Major: breaking schema or permission changes.
Every release carries current version, prior version, changes, affected modules, source/test receipts and reversible migration instructions. Export/backup customer data first. Add fields and map stable IDs; never replace their recordings, costs, artist preferences or custom views automatically. Old versions remain retrievable.

## Proof and launch admission

Required: complete private personal production cycle; clean customer duplication/install; mobile first action; 10/50/100-record usability; no private backlinks; accurate feature/prerequisite statement; rights/license review; selected price/merchant/tax/refund/support terms; checkout and delivery test; real buyer activation observations.

Prepare a 14-day demand test: five independent creators complete the first workflow, at most eight founder hours of batch support and no ad spend unless separately authorized. Record friction, actual support time and willingness to pay. Do not claim all five participated until receipts exist.

Music success proof and product proof remain separate. Publish a dated case study with stage, denominator, window, costs and limits. No fabricated testimonials, playlist promises or revenue screenshots.
