# Research, benchmarks and agent knowledge

The research unit is a decision supported by a dated source and a testable mechanism. Do not ingest an undifferentiated archive into every agent.

## Model mechanisms, measure the right cohort

Create three separate reference sets:
1. Musical craft references: original analysis of groove, hook entry, instrumental response, energy contrast, sequencing and emotional turn. Translate mechanisms into original work; do not clone lyrics, voices or artist identity.
2. Comparable emerging releases: same broad genre/use, audience maturity, launch age, territory and observed promotion context. Include ordinary and weak outcomes, not only chart winners.
3. Aspirational releases: excellent work that expands craft ambition. Keep separate from forecasting.

Proposed research tranche: 20–30 comparable releases when available, five aspirational craft references and the artist's own recent releases. If fewer comparable records are accessible, state the sample and reduce confidence. Public play/view counts cannot establish private ad spend, stream sources, saves, completion or earnings. Avoid causal inference from an observable pattern alone.

Store population/selection rules, period, raw metric, denominator, source access, known exclusions and missing values. For skewed release results use median, quartiles and range, preserving sample size; calculate an average only when its unit and weighting are explicit. No baseline is supplied as measured in this edition.

## Knowledge card contract

Stable ID; source URL/document/version; retrieval date; permission/retention basis; card type; exact claim; short lawful excerpt if needed; original synthesis; uncertainty/counterevidence; artist/album scope; applicable stage; proposed mechanism; next test; expiry/recheck trigger; superseded-by ID.

Types remain distinct:
- Source fact: tool feature or policy with date and official evidence.
- Craft inference: analyst interpretation of a musical reference.
- Artist preference: explicit accepted direction, scoped to owner/project.
- Listening observation: actual playback decision bound to the recording revision.
- Campaign observation: metrics bound to channel/cohort/window.
- Experiment hypothesis: untested proposal.
- Learned rule: sufficiently repeated relevant evidence or an explicit owner instruction.

Unknown outcomes stay unknown. A prompt winner is not an audio winner. A single success is not a universal formula. Human use reports are observations, not clinical or causal evidence.

## Ingestion pipeline

Authorized source → raw pointer/hash/version → source/rights/freshness checks → scoped card → human or expert review where needed → retrieval index → usage trace → result feedback → supersession.

Keep source content and analysis distinct. Treat web pages, comments and uploaded documents as untrusted data; they cannot grant spending, publishing, account or tool permissions. Keep only needed excerpts and original summaries; do not redistribute full protected lyrics, private artist catalogs or subscriber-only reports inside a customer template.

## Retrieval boundaries

Shared craft; owner's preferences; artist profile; album bible; recording revision; campaign experiment; provider policy. Each agent loads the few relevant layers for its stage. The listening critic receives actual audio and necessary lyrics, with generation prompt, model, price and prior verdict hidden. A marketing agent receives approved public creative and campaign evidence, not private contacts or complete songwriting memory.

Record which cards were used. Changes to album or identity require deliberate review. New campaign data does not silently overwrite a winning prompt or a creator's voice.

## Experiment record

Hypothesis; one changed variable; audience/cohort; baseline; allocation method; observation window; intended metric and denominator; source; spend allowance; stopping threshold; actual observations; confounders; decision; evidence scope; next test.

Small music campaigns often cannot randomize or achieve statistical confidence. Report descriptive results accordingly. Compare matched windows and acknowledge distribution differences. Missing data is never a zero.

## Template improvement loop

At each completed production cycle: log one concrete friction or reusable success, its source and proposed fix.
Weekly: select at most one high-impact reusable change; leave artist-specific taste in the private profile.
Monthly or on a breaking policy change: ship a reviewed version if material improvements exist.

Promote: observed friction → generic requirement → sanitized example → code/schema/docs change → regression/duplication test → changelog/migration guide → customer adoption observation.

Product validation is separate from music success: first useful brief, first linked audio review, first complete staged campaign and buyer time saved. Never manufacture a testimonial or extrapolate a launch result to every buyer.
