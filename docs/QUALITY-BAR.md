# Quality Bar

## Pass condition

No Suno generation before:
- weighted taste score ≥85/100 for songs/instrumentals or ≥90/100 for meditations;
- every axis ≥7.5/10;
- no originality, safety, completeness, or contradiction veto;
- deterministic session validation passes.

## Song room checklist

### Premise
- The speaker, listener, and pressure are identifiable.
- The ending sentence was impossible at the beginning.
- The song is about an event/action, not only a mood label.

### Language
- One coherent image system develops across sections.
- Concrete nouns and active verbs carry the feeling.
- Vocabulary is exact and singable, not ornamental.
- No cliché survives without a specific transformation.
- No named-artist signature, borrowed lyric, or near-paraphrase.

### Hook and form
- The title phrase is clear on first listen and richer after Verse 2.
- Verse 2 provides new evidence or consequence.
- Bridge reveals, reverses, or charges a cost.
- Final chorus changes meaning or arrangement because the story changed.

### Prosody
- Stresses align with musical emphasis.
- Parallel lines have compatible rhythmic shapes.
- Held notes land on singable vowels.
- Rhyme mixes perfect, family, internal, and slant relationships.
- Read-aloud stumbles are repaired.

### Production
- Genre, rhythm, harmony, timbre, vocal persona, mix, and ending serve one thesis.
- The energy map contains contrast and negative space.
- The style prompt names 3–6 focal instruments, not an orchestra of keywords.
- Exclusions protect the thesis rather than negate the prompt.

## Guided meditation checklist

- Production lane is explicit: separate narration/bed or experimental spoken Suno.
- Duration estimate matches word count and silence.
- Opening provides agency, orientation, and optional eyes/posture.
- One primary technique is used.
- Instructions are sequential and speakable.
- Pauses are written into the script.
- Breath changes are gentle and optional; no retention is prescribed.
- No diagnosis, treatment claim, trauma excavation, coercion, or guaranteed outcome.
- Return widens attention safely, or sleep lane fades without demanding wakefulness.
- Instrumental bed protects speech intelligibility and avoids sudden events.

## First-listen A&R

For each generated take, answer:
1. What is the first unmistakable identity signal?
2. Does the hook arrive with enough setup and enough contrast?
3. Which word or phrase is mispronounced or emotionally underplayed?
4. Where does arrangement density mask the lyric or narration?
5. Does the bridge/turn change the listening state?
6. Does the ending feel authored?
7. What accidental moment should be preserved?

Verdict: `KEEP`, `ITERATE`, or `CUT`, with one next move.

Do not report technical audio measurements without running an actual analyzer.
